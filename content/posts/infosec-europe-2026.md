It’s been a few years since I last attended Infosec Europe, so I was super excited to return. This time round, I decided to focus my time on two things: workshops where I could take a deeper dive with leading experts, and a handful of vendors I wanted to learn more about.

Below is a quick summary, in no particular order:

**Workshops Attended**  
- Post-Quantum Cryptography by **SANS**  
- Learnt about various types of DDoS attacks and protections with **Mazebolt**  
- Pentest platform demo with the **Pentera** team  
- TTX with **N-able**

**Some of the Vendors I Connected With**  
- **Recorded Future** - they do a bunch, but I was interested in brand protection  
- **Push Security** - browser protection via a browser extension  
- **Abnormal** - a more intelligent email security solution  
- **Netskope** - multilayer protection, including application-aware solutions
